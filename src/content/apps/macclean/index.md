> Native macOS disk cleaner for WeChat/QQ/WeCom caches, Storage Map, and Xcode/AI/dev caches — review before delete.

MacClean scans WeChat media, System Data, Xcode caches, and local AI model folders, then lets you review items before anything goes to Trash. Scanning stays available; cleanup after the trial needs a one-time Lifetime (¥98) or Family license. Not Apple-notarized yet. Install from [thulem.com](https://thulem.com/) or `brew install --cask jinglings/macclean/macclean`.

> Instant file search for macOS, in the spirit of Everything for Windows: results update on every keystroke across internal and external disks, including disks that are not connected.

Espresso is a native AppKit app with a Finder look and feel. It has a full search syntax, saved searches, and Blends that search several disks together. External disks stay searchable after you unplug them. Names, the index and the on-device model behind Ask the Barista never leave your Mac, and there is no analytics or telemetry.

Espresso Free is free forever for personal, non-commercial use and includes two offline disks. Espresso Pro is a one-time €19 purchase that covers every update on up to three Macs, with a 14-day trial. Pro adds unlimited offline disks, extended metadata, bulk rename, CSV export, search inside files, the Timeline, and automation through the command line, Shortcuts, Raycast, Alfred and MCP. Pro is required for use at work. Espresso needs macOS 14 or later and also installs with Homebrew (`brew install mokasolutions/tap/espresso-search`).

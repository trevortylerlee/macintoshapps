> Focuh is a free Mac app (plus a web app) for planning Today, the week, loops, and goals — and blocking distracting websites system-wide during a focus session.

It is built for the creatively chaotic: dated work on a week board, a short Today next to your calendar, standing intentions in Loops, and one win per horizon in Goals. On Mac, Accessibility-based blocking covers every browser for the length of a focus session. The same Clerk account syncs the planner to the web app.

**Pricing:** 100% free. No premium tier.

**Download:** Apple Silicon and Intel DMGs at https://www.focuh.com/download (v0.3.0, early beta, signed and notarized). Web app: https://www.focuh.com

**Requirements:** macOS 12+. Free account for Mac and web sync. Blocking is Mac-only during a focus session.

Bass Plus adds a bass dial, volume boost, and an equalizer to the Mac menu bar. It works across apps, so you can adjust the same headphones or speakers while switching between music, video, and games.

Bass Plus requires macOS 14.4 or later. The Mac App Store download includes a seven-day full trial. Continued use costs $14.99 as a one-time purchase in the US, with no subscription.

Mac App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bass-plus/id6818408560

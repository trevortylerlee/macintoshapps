> Unspoken turns speech into polished text in the Mac app you are already using, with speech processing running locally on your device.

It is built for people who write a lot of emails, notes, support replies, CRM updates, proposals, or drafts and want a private Mac workflow instead of sending voice to a cloud dictation service. Unspoken supports macOS 14 and later, with Apple Silicon and Intel builds available.

Orca One is AI dictation and meeting notes for Mac. It’s fast, runs on device, and completely private. Dictate into Mac apps and capture meeting transcripts without adding a bot to the call.

Orca One runs transcription on Apple silicon Macs. It also includes a voice launcher. You can try it for free, then buy a one-time license for one Mac with no subscription. It requires macOS 15 or later.

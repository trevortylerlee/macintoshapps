> Yappr puts your AI agents (Claude Code, Codex) in the MacBook notch. When one needs you, the notch shows which session is asking and what, and you answer there by click or voice. It also does push-to-talk dictation: hold a key, talk, and cleaned text lands at the cursor in any app.

Apple silicon only, macOS 15 or later, closed source. Free covers plain dictation, 2,000 words a week. Pro is $10/month (founder price) with a 14-day trial, and the first 50 people get it free forever when they share it with 2 friends.

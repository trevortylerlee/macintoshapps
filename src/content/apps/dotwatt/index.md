> Know how long your MacBook lasts and what drains it. Dotwatt replaces the battery icon in the menu bar with measured time left, the apps spending the charge, an 80% charge limit and Low Power in one click.

It also keeps the Mac awake while coding agents such as Claude Code, Codex and Cursor work, records every night asleep and battery health month by month, and shows processor, memory, disk, network, temperatures and fans. Native Swift, no account and no telemetry. Requires Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later.

Dotwatt is a one-time purchase with a 7-day free trial, sold directly from [its website](https://dotwatt.app/). One key covers up to three Macs.

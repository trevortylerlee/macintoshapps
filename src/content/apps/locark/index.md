> A local-first personal workspace with free Markdown notes, password records and Inbox; optional Pro unlocks kanban-board and mind-map creation.

LocArk stores workspaces in a location you choose, and core features work offline. New workspaces are locally encrypted and protected by a master password. LocArk does not provide hosted cloud sync; you can choose a third-party sync folder. It does not include AI features, team collaboration or browser/system autofill.

The Mac app requires macOS 12.0 or later, supports Intel Macs and runs natively on Apple silicon without Rosetta. It is distributed through the [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/locark-private-workspace/id6762795586).

[Website pricing](https://locark.com/pricing/) is free for notes, password records and Inbox, with optional Pro at US$4.90/year or US$24.90 lifetime. App-store prices and purchase terms may differ. See the [official press kit](https://locark.com/press/) for product facts and assets.

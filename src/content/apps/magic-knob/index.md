> Rest two fingers on the trackpad and rotate — volume or brightness follows like a real knob.

Magic Knob is a native macOS menu bar app that maps the trackpad's two-finger rotation gesture to a precision dial for volume or brightness, with a native glass HUD. A three-finger click plays/pauses or triggers keyboard shortcuts, media keys, or the lock screen. Adjustable sensitivity with a velocity-sensitive curve: slow turns are precise, fast turns move more.

It works with built-in MacBook trackpads and the Magic Trackpad, runs fully locally with no account or telemetry, and costs $9 as a one-time purchase after a free 14-day trial.

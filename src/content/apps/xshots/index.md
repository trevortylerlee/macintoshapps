XShots is a native Mac app for designing complete App Store screenshot sets on a continuous canvas. Reuse templates and store-size presets, manage localized copy, preview listings, and batch-export PNG or JPEG across sizes and languages. Projects and source images stay on your Mac. An optional local MCP server works with compatible clients, whose own privacy settings apply.

Requires macOS 14 or later; Apple-powered automatic translation requires macOS 15 or later. A one-time three-day full trial is available. Paid access is a non-renewing 7-Day Pass (US App Store $0.99) or Pro Lifetime ($19.99); regional store prices may differ. Existing projects remain readable when time-limited access ends. See the official [press kit](https://selfark.com/xshots/press/).

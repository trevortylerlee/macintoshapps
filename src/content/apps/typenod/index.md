TypeNod is a Mac input method for Simplified Chinese Pinyin, English, Spanish and French. Preview an on-device translation while typing, then confirm insertion into the current text field.

Apple's translation resources must be downloaded first; supported language pairs vary. Supported translation works offline after setup. Requires macOS 26 or later on Intel or Apple silicon; sentence refinement requires 26.4 or later. Distributed directly as a signed, notarized DMG.

US$9.90 including tax buys a lifetime Mac license for one person on every Mac they use, including future Mac app updates. No free trial; a 30-day full refund is available. Future macOS compatibility is not guaranteed. See the official [press kit](https://typenod.com/press/) and [installation and licensing details](https://typenod.com/install/).

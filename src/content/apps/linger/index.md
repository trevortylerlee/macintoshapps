Linger is a local-first visual reference library for designers and artists. It keeps images, screenshots, notes, tags, and source links together on your Mac. Find saved references by text or color, then arrange them side by side on a project canvas.

It runs on Apple Silicon with macOS 14 or later. A seven-day trial is available without an account, and a one-time license is available from the developer's website.

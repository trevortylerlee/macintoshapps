> Brink turns the edges of your MacBook trackpad into controls. Slide along the right edge for volume, the left for brightness and the top for keyboard light; swipe the bottom to skip a track, or double-tap the top-right corner to mute.

It shows the same HUD as the keyboard keys, ticks under your finger with haptics as you slide, and keeps the pointer still while you do it. Hold Shift and Option for quarter steps, choose where slides can start, swap sides, or require a modifier key. It works with the Magic Trackpad and Magic Mouse too.

Brink is free, universal (Apple silicon and Intel), signed and notarized, and updates itself. It needs macOS 13 or later.

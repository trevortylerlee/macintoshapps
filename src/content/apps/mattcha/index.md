> MattCha is a free, native Mattermost client for iPhone, iPad, and Mac, built for threaded conversations, Markdown, voice messages, and your own server.

On Mac, MattCha provides a native three-column workspace with a sidebar, channel view, and optional thread pane. It connects directly to a compatible Mattermost server without creating a separate MattCha account, and includes no analytics, advertising identifiers, telemetry, or tracking SDKs.

MattCha is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Mattermost, Inc.

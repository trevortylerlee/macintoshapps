> Abendrot is a free, open-source Mac screen warmer that cuts nighttime blue light.

At night, you need to reduce your blue light exposure. Abendrot targets the root cause of sleep disruption by minimizing the specific short-wavelength, melanopic light (blue/cyan) that tricks your body clock into thinking it's still daytime. It warms every display around your local sunset.

No account, no telemetry, MIT licensed. [abendrot.app](https://abendrot.app) · [GitHub](https://github.com/matthewrball/abendrot)

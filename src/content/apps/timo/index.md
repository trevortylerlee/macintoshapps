> Timo is a clipboard manager and productivity toolkit for macOS 14.6 or later. It keeps text, images, links, and files available in searchable clipboard history, and also includes app, file, and folder search, on-device OCR, screenshot capture and annotation, notes, calculations, iCloud sync, reusable fill groups, and an optional desktop companion.

Timo supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs. It starts free, with an optional $19.99 lifetime upgrade available from the official website.

> Wow Keywords is a desktop app for App Store Optimization on Mac and Windows. It tracks keywords in 52 countries, drafts your listing and screenshot headings in 50 languages with AI, and uploads them to App Store Connect or Google Play.

It's a one-time purchase of $99 with no subscription. It runs on the AI tools you already use, and includes an MCP server.
